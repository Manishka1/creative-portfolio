A Little About Me

I'm interested in why some things stay with us.

I've always been interested in why certain ideas, scenes, jokes, or posts stay in our minds long after we've seen them.

Over the last four years, that little corner grew into an engaged community of 26,800+ followers, generating over 310,000 monthly impressions. Handling the entire process independently — from initial concept research and caption copywriting to graphic layouts, video editing, and audience analytics — gave me an end-to-end understanding of what makes creative communication work.

Running the page taught me much more than just social media. I've learned how to find ideas, write for an audience, turn thoughts into visuals, edit videos, work with brands, and understand what makes people connect with something.

Alongside content creation, I've been developing my own voice as a writer, particularly around cinema, pop culture, comedy, creators, and internet culture.
